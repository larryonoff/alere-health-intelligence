# Alere: Health Intelligence — Vision

## Purpose

Alere helps people make better everyday nutrition decisions using credible
evidence, personal context, and practical guidance.

It is not a diet program, a food-ranking system, or a replacement for medical
care. Its role is to turn complex and sometimes conflicting nutrition
information into clear choices a person can realistically use.

## Current scope

Alere currently exists as one evolving AI skill focused on:

- answering nutrition questions;
- evaluating meals and eating patterns;
- suggesting realistic improvements and substitutions;
- explaining the strength and limitations of relevant evidence;
- adapting guidance to goals, preferences, culture, budget, and constraints.

## Direction

The immediate product goal is a minimal native iOS application built around one
loop: goal, conversation, one practical action, feedback, and weekly synthesis.

The skill remains the implementation-independent definition of assistant
behavior. Product interface, persistence, system integrations, and provider
routing belong outside the skill.

## Boundaries

Alere should remain evidence-based, transparent about uncertainty, respectful
of user autonomy, and careful in medical or otherwise high-risk situations.
