# Knowledge Changelog

Record runtime knowledge changes, not every editorial candidate.

Each entry should state:

- date and affected evidence or practical IDs;
- whether the change confirms, narrows, contradicts, supersedes, or withdraws
  an earlier conclusion;
- the decisive source identifiers;
- changes to strength, applicability, safety boundaries, or practical advice;
- evaluation and clinical-review status.
