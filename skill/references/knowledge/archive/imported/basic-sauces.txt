# Basic creamy seed or nut sauce

## Cashew version

Ingredients:

- cashews;
- lemon juice;
- salt;
- water as needed.

Preparation:

1. Cover the cashews with boiling water and leave for 20 minutes.
2. Drain and blend with lemon juice and salt.
3. Add water gradually until the desired consistency is reached.

## Seed substitutions

Cashews can be replaced with sunflower or sesame seeds. Soak the seeds
overnight before blending. Exact quantities were not stated in the source, so
adjust lemon, salt, and water gradually rather than presenting invented amounts.
