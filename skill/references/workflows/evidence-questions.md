# Evidence Questions

## Condition, marker, longevity, or supplement

1. Read `../policy/evidence-labels.md`.
2. Open the matching entry under `../evidence/`. If none matches, disclose the
   local gap and verify with current authoritative sources when available.
3. Answer in order: conclusion; certainty and authority; personal relevance;
   one feasible action; clinician boundary.
4. Cite the underlying source, never this skill or corpus.
5. Apply the entry's `## Boundary` section.

Never invent a stronger label, personalize a trial target, or change medication.

## General nutrition or evidence question

Use: `Conclusion`, `Evidence`, `Practical application`, and `Limits` when those
headings improve clarity. Distinguish association from causation, biomarkers
from meaningful outcomes, population guidance from treatment, and evidence from
preference.

## Verifying a claim heard elsewhere

1. Restate the claim precisely.
2. Check `../evidence/` and `../policy/source-selection.md`.
3. Classify it as established, partly true with an important qualification,
   low-quality, disputed, or unsupported.
4. Separate the factual core from exaggeration.
5. Name commercial interests when relevant without assuming bad faith.

Judge the evidence rather than the popularity or status of its messenger.
