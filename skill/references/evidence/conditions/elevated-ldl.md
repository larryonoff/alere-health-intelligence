# Elevated LDL cholesterol

One of the most quantifiable areas in nutrition: specific dietary changes produce
measurable, replicated LDL reductions. That precision is the reason to be careful
not to overstate what it means for an individual.

## Claims

### The Portfolio dietary pattern lowers LDL substantially

**Strength: strong.**

The Portfolio Diet combines four cholesterol-lowering components on a
low-saturated-fat base: viscous soluble fibre, plant protein, nuts, and plant
sterols. A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials
found a mean LDL reduction of approximately **17%**; controlled feeding studies
combining all components report reductions approaching 30%.

- Chiavaroli L et al. Portfolio Dietary Pattern and Cardiovascular Disease: A
  Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Controlled Trials. *Prog Cardiovasc Dis*
  2018;61(1):43–53.
  DOI: [10.1016/j.pcad.2018.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pcad.2018.05.004).
  PMID: [29807048](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29807048/).
- Glenn AJ et al. Portfolio Diet Score and Risk of Cardiovascular Disease.
  *Circulation* 2023;148(22):1750–1763.
  DOI: [10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065551](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065551).
  PMID: [37877288](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37877288/).
- American College of Cardiology. Dietary Approaches For Elevated LDL-C. 2025.
  <https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Articles/2025/07/01/01/Prioritizing-Health-Dietary-Approaches-For-Elevated-LDL-C>

### Replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fat lowers LDL

**Strength: strong.**

Replacing approximately 5% of total energy from saturated fat with
polyunsaturated fat is associated with roughly 5–10% LDL reduction. Comprehensive
dietary intervention trials report mean LDL reductions of 13–20%.

- Dietary Therapy for LDL Cholesterol Reduction: Evidence-Based Patterns for
  Cardiovascular Risk Management. *StatPearls*, NCBI Bookshelf.
  <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK551722/>
- Hooper L et al. Reduction in saturated fat intake for cardiovascular disease.
  *Cochrane Database Syst Rev* 2020;8:CD011737.
  DOI: [10.1002/14651858.CD011737.pub3](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD011737.pub3).
  PMID: [32827219](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32827219/).

Note the wording: **replacement**, not removal. What saturated fat is replaced
with determines the effect.

### Guidelines recommend specific dietary patterns for cardiovascular risk

**Strength: strong.**

The 2025 AACE consensus statement recommends Mediterranean, DASH, and healthful
plant-based dietary patterns to reduce atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease
risk.

- American Association of Clinical Endocrinology consensus statement on dietary
  patterns and cardiometabolic risk, 2025. Verify the current version before
  quoting a specific recommendation.

### Diet magnitude can approach statin magnitude — with important caveats

**Strength: moderate.**

Full Portfolio adherence on a low-saturated-fat base can lower LDL by roughly
30%, a magnitude comparable to a moderate-intensity statin. But:

- comparability is in LDL change, **not** in demonstrated event reduction;
- statins have large randomized outcome trials; dietary patterns have far fewer;
- the 30% figure comes from controlled feeding, where adherence is far higher than
  in free-living use.

Never present this as grounds for declining or discontinuing prescribed therapy.

## Boundary

- **Never suggest stopping, reducing, or avoiding lipid-lowering medication.**
  Framing diet as a statin alternative is outside scope and potentially harmful.
- Do not interpret a lipid panel, calculate cardiovascular risk, or set a personal
  LDL target. Targets depend on overall risk, family history, and conditions a
  clinician assesses.
- Familial hypercholesterolaemia is a genetic condition requiring specialist care;
  route rather than advise.
- Plant sterol supplements are a supplement-dosing question — route per
  `../../safety-and-context.md`.
- Route pregnancy, existing cardiovascular disease, and diabetes for individual
  care.

## How to use this entry

This is a topic where a concrete, numeric answer is genuinely available — give
it. One component (for example, oats or barley for viscous fibre, or nuts) is a
feasible single step, and the components are additive.
