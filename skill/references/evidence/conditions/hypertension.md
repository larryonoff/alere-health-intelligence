# Hypertension and elevated blood pressure

The DASH trials are among the strongest dietary intervention evidence in
existence: a defined eating pattern, tested in randomized controlled feeding
studies, with blood pressure as a hard, measurable outcome.

## Claims

### The DASH dietary pattern lowers blood pressure

**Strength: strong.**

The original randomized controlled feeding trial found that a diet rich in
fruits, vegetables, and low-fat dairy with reduced saturated and total fat
significantly lowered blood pressure compared with a control diet, over 8 weeks,
without weight loss or sodium reduction as the mechanism.

- Appel LJ et al. A clinical trial of the effects of dietary patterns on blood
  pressure. *N Engl J Med* 1997;336(16):1117–1124.
  DOI: [10.1056/NEJM199704173361601](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199704173361601).
  PMID: [9099655](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9099655/).
- The DASH Diet: A Guide to Managing Hypertension Through Nutrition.
  *StatPearls*, NCBI Bookshelf. <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482514/>

### Sodium reduction adds a further, dose-dependent effect

**Strength: strong.**

The DASH-Sodium trial showed blood pressure fell progressively as sodium intake
was reduced, and the effects of the DASH pattern and sodium reduction were
additive.

- Sacks FM et al. Effects on blood pressure of reduced dietary sodium and the
  Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) diet. *N Engl J Med*
  2001;344(1):3–10.
  DOI: [10.1056/NEJM200101043440101](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM200101043440101).
  PMID: [11136953](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11136953/).
- World Health Organization. Guideline: Sodium intake for adults and children.
  2012. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241504836>

### Guidelines place dietary change alongside pharmacological treatment

**Strength: strong.**

The 2025 AHA/ACC hypertension guideline maintains a 130/80 mmHg diagnostic and
treatment threshold and combines pharmacological therapy with lifestyle
intervention: dietary modification, physical activity, weight management, and
smoking cessation.

- 2025 AHA/ACC hypertension guideline. See summary and implications:
  <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12379440/>
  Verify the current guideline text before quoting a threshold or target.

### Modified and digitally delivered DASH interventions retain effect

**Strength: moderate.**

A systematic review and meta-analysis of modified DASH diets in adults with
elevated blood pressure or hypertension found significant blood pressure
reduction. A 2025 scoping review of digitally delivered DASH interventions found
favourable effects on blood pressure and dietary sodium.

- Effects of the Modified DASH Diet on Adults With Elevated Blood Pressure or
  Hypertension: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. *Front Nutr* 2021.
  <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8452928/>
- Digital-Based Nutrition Interventions Employing the DASH Diet: A Systematic
  Scoping Review. 2025.
  <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12413951/>

Relevant to Alere directly: digital delivery of this specific pattern has
supporting evidence.

## Boundary

- **Never advise on antihypertensive medication** — starting, stopping, dose, or
  timing.
- Do not interpret blood pressure readings, diagnose hypertension, or set a
  personal target. The 130/80 threshold is a guideline definition, not an
  individual prescription.
- Potassium is central to the DASH pattern, but **potassium restriction applies in
  chronic kidney disease and with certain medications** (ACE inhibitors, ARBs,
  potassium-sparing diuretics). Never recommend increasing potassium-rich foods
  without checking for kidney disease and current medication — route instead.
- Route pregnancy (including pre-eclampsia risk), chronic kidney disease, heart
  failure, and any acute symptoms per `../../safety-and-context.md`.

## How to use this entry

DASH is a pattern, not a prescription: describe its shape and let the person adopt
one element. Sodium reduction and increased vegetable and fruit intake are the two
highest-yield single steps for most people — subject to the kidney and medication
check above.
