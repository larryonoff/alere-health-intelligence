# MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease)

Formerly NAFLD. Unusual in nutrition for having **threshold-specific** outcome
evidence: defined amounts of weight loss map to defined histological changes.

Nomenclature note: MASLD replaced NAFLD in 2023 multi-society consensus. Older
literature uses NAFLD/NASH; MASH is the current term for the steatohepatitis
form. Treat the terms as continuous when citing older evidence, and say so.

- Rinella ME et al. A multisociety Delphi consensus statement on new fatty liver
  disease nomenclature. *Hepatology* 2023;78(6):1966–1986.
  DOI: [10.1097/HEP.0000000000000520](https://doi.org/10.1097/HEP.0000000000000520).
  PMID: [37363821](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37363821/).

## Claims

### Weight loss produces dose-dependent histological improvement

**Strength: strong.**

Thresholds, from a prospective study with paired biopsies and reflected in AGA
clinical guidance:

| Weight loss | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| ≥3–5% | reduction in hepatic steatosis |
| ≥7% | resolution of steatohepatitis (NASH/MASH) |
| ≥10% | fibrosis regression or stability |

- Vilar-Gomez E et al. Weight loss through lifestyle modification significantly
  reduces features of nonalcoholic steatohepatitis. *Gastroenterology*
  2015;149(2):367–378.
  DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2015.04.005](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2015.04.005).
  PMID: [25865049](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25865049/).
- American Gastroenterological Association. Lifestyle modification using diet and
  exercise to achieve weight loss in the management of NAFLD.
  <https://gastro.org/clinical-guidance/lifestyle-modification-using-diet-and-exercise-to-achieve-weight-loss-in-the-management-of-nonalcoholic-fatty-liver-disease-nafld/>
- Practical Lifestyle Management of Nonalcoholic Fatty Liver Disease for Busy
  Clinicians. *Diabetes Spectr* 2024;37(1):39.
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/spectrum/article/37/1/39/153914/>

This is the clearest "how much matters" answer in the corpus. Use the specific
threshold relevant to what the person has been told, not the largest number.

### Lifestyle modification is the primary established management

**Strength: strong.**

Weight loss achieved through a Mediterranean-style eating pattern combined with
regular physical activity is the most evidence-based management for MASLD.

- European Association for the Study of the Liver. EASL–EASD–EASO Clinical
  Practice Guidelines on the management of MASLD. *J Hepatol* 2024.
  DOI: [10.1016/j.jhep.2024.04.031](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhep.2024.04.031).
  PMID: [38851997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38851997/).

### Weight regain reverses the benefit at a small margin

**Strength: moderate.**

An analysis in men identified regain of approximately **1.5 kg** as the cutoff
associated with recurrence. Regular exercisers were more represented in the
sustained-remission group (34.6%) than the recurrence group (24.5%).

- Weight regain of 1.5 kg or more and lack of exercise are associated with
  nonalcoholic fatty liver disease recurrence in men. 2021.
  <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8497533/>

Observational, male-only, single-cohort — hence `moderate`. The practical point
is that maintenance matters as much as the initial loss, which changes what a
useful next action looks like.

### Steatosis can improve at normal body weight with modest loss

**Strength: weak.**

Lean MASLD may improve with 3–5% body-weight reduction plus increased physical
activity. Evidence in lean populations is thinner than in overweight and obese
populations.

- Long MT, Noureddin M, Lim JK. AGA Clinical Practice Update: Diagnosis and
  Management of NAFLD in Lean Individuals. *Gastroenterology* 2022.
  DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2022.06.023](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2022.06.023).

## Boundary

- Diagnosis requires imaging, laboratory tests, or biopsy. Never infer MASLD from
  symptoms, weight, or diet.
- Do not interpret liver enzymes, elastography, fibrosis scores (FIB-4, NFS), or
  imaging reports.
- Alcohol intake is central to differential diagnosis and management. Do not set
  personal alcohol limits; state that this belongs with the treating clinician.
- Do not recommend rapid weight loss. Excessively rapid loss can worsen liver
  inflammation, and rate belongs under medical supervision.
- Never recommend supplements marketed for liver health — including vitamin E,
  which has trial evidence in specific populations but is a dosing and safety
  decision requiring a clinician.
- Route advanced fibrosis, cirrhosis, pregnancy, and any hepatology follow-up per
  `../../safety-and-context.md`.

## How to use this entry

The thresholds are the value here — they turn a vague goal into a concrete one,
and they show that a modest amount is genuinely worthwhile. Pair the relevant
threshold with one sustainable step, and mention that maintenance is where the
benefit is kept or lost.
