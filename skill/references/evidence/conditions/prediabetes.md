# Prediabetes

Elevated glucose below the diabetes threshold. Diagnosis and monitoring belong to
a clinician; dietary and activity change is where a person has agency.

## Claims

### Structured lifestyle change substantially reduces progression to type 2 diabetes

**Strength: strong.**

The Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP) randomized trial found intensive lifestyle
intervention reduced incident type 2 diabetes by **58% over 3 years**, and by
**71% in adults aged 60 and over**.

- Knowler WC et al. Reduction in the incidence of type 2 diabetes with lifestyle
  intervention or metformin. *N Engl J Med* 2002;346(6):393–403.
  DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa012512](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512).
  PMID: [11832527](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11832527/).

### The effect is durable over decades

**Strength: strong.**

Long-term follow-up of three independent trials shows sustained risk reduction:

| Study | Follow-up | Reduction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Da Qing | 30 years | 39% |
| Finnish DPS | 7 years | 43% |
| DPPOS (US) | 10 / 15 / 21 years | 34% / 27% / 24% |

- Gong Q et al. Morbidity and mortality after lifestyle intervention for people
  with impaired glucose tolerance: 30-year results of the Da Qing Diabetes
  Prevention Outcome Study. *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol* 2019;7(6):452–461.
  DOI: [10.1016/S2213-8587(19)30093-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(19)30093-2).
  PMID: [31036503](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31036503/).
- Lindström J et al. Sustained reduction in the incidence of type 2 diabetes by
  lifestyle intervention: follow-up of the Finnish Diabetes Prevention Study.
  *Lancet* 2006;368(9548):1673–1679.
  DOI: [10.1016/S0140-6736(06)69701-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(06)69701-8).
  PMID: [17098085](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17098085/).
- Diabetes Prevention Program Research Group. The Diabetes Prevention Program and
  its Outcomes Study: NIDDK's journey into the prevention of type 2 diabetes.
  *Diabetes Care* 2025;48(7):1101.
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/48/7/1101/158195/>

### Guidelines direct that such programs be offered

**Strength: strong.**

The ADA Standards of Care recommend referring adults at increased risk of type 2
diabetes to intensive lifestyle behavior-change programs modeled on the DPP.

- American Diabetes Association. 3. Prevention or Delay of Diabetes and
  Associated Comorbidities: *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026*.
  *Diabetes Care* 2026;49(Suppl 1):S50.
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S50/163924/>
  (Revised annually — check the current supplement before quoting a number.)

### Scale of the population

**Strength: strong.**

Global prediabetes prevalence is rising: impaired glucose tolerance from 9.1% to
12.0% and impaired fasting glucose from 5.8% to 9.2% between 2021 and 2024.
Projections exceed 470 million people by 2030.

- Rooney MR et al. Global prevalence of prediabetes. *Diabetes Care*
  2023;46(7):1388–1394.
  DOI: [10.2337/dc22-2376](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc22-2376).
  PMID: [37196350](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37196350/).
- Rooney MR et al. Global and regional prediabetes prevalence: updates for 2024
  and projections for 2050. *Diabetes Care* 2025;48(11):e142.
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/48/11/e142/163407/>

### What the intervention actually consisted of

**Strength: strong** (this is a description of trial protocol, not a claim of
effect).

DPP's lifestyle arm targeted at least 7% body-weight loss and at least 150
minutes per week of moderate physical activity, delivered through structured,
supported behavior change — not a diet handed over once.

Practical reading: the effective ingredients were sustained modest weight loss,
regular activity, and ongoing support. No specific branded diet was required.

## Boundary

- Diagnosis, glucose testing, and interpretation of results belong to a
  clinician. Do not diagnose prediabetes from symptoms or self-report.
- Metformin was a separate DPP arm and is a medication decision. Never recommend,
  adjust, or discuss starting it as advice.
- Do not convert the 7% and 150-minute trial targets into a personal prescription
  for someone with cardiac, musculoskeletal, pregnancy-related, or other
  limitations.
- Route to `../../safety-and-context.md` for pregnancy, eating-disorder signals,
  significant symptoms, rapid unintentional weight change, or existing chronic
  disease.

## How to use this entry

Lead with the fact that this is one of the best-evidenced areas in nutrition —
the person genuinely has leverage. Then pick one feasible action, not the whole
protocol. Related: `type-2-diabetes.md`, `../longevity/dietary-patterns.md`.
