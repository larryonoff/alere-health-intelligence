# Editorial Source Backlog

This is a maintenance index, not a source of claims for user responses. Open
and verify the current underlying document before promotion or citation.

For where to search and which endpoint to use, see `skill/references/sources/registry.md` —
it carries the identifier-verification APIs, the Cambridge nutrition journals, the
named-researcher route, and per-source access notes. The verification procedure
itself is `docs/verification-playbook.md`.

## Core nutrition

### World Health Organization

- Healthy diet:
  <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet>
- Use for adequacy, balance, moderation, diversity, food groups, and
  population-level numerical guidance.
- Recheck publication date and the linked guideline before using a number.

### Nordic Nutrition Recommendations 2023

- Report: <https://pub.norden.org/nord2023-003/>
- Use for European dietary patterns, food groups, nutrient reference values,
  and sustainability.
- Track replacement by a later edition.

### EFSA

- Dietary reference values:
  <https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/dietary-reference-values>
- Food supplements:
  <https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/food-supplements>
- Use for European terminology, population reference points, tolerable upper
  intake levels, and supplement-safety context.

### Poland

- Talerz Zdrowego Żywienia:
  <https://ncez.pzh.gov.pl/abc-zywienia/talerz-zdrowego-zywienia/>
- Use for Polish food-based communication and regional practicality.
- Add specialist Polish sources only when a supported user need appears.

## Lifestyle foundations

### World Health Organization — physical activity

- Guidelines:
  <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128>
- Use for population-level activity and sedentary-behaviour guidance.
- Do not turn it into individualized training for disease, injury, pregnancy,
  or disability.

### Sleep

- AASM/SRS adult sleep-duration consensus:
  <https://aasm.org/resources/pdf/adultsleepdurationconsensus.pdf>
- Use for general adult sleep opportunity.
- Add newer European or international guidance when it materially improves
  applicability.

## Restriction and meal timing

### National Institute on Aging

- Calorie restriction and fasting overview:
  <https://www.nia.nih.gov/news/calorie-restriction-and-fasting-diets-what-do-we-know>
- Use to distinguish experimental longevity hypotheses from established human
  outcomes.

### CALERIE

- Trial analysis:
  <https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-022-00357-y>
- Check the primary trial design, achieved restriction, selected biomarkers,
  adverse effects, and lack of direct mortality evidence before summarizing.

### Intermittent fasting

- 2025 BMJ network meta-analysis:
  <https://www.bmj.com/content/389/bmj-2024-082007>
- Recheck population, intervention definitions, duration, comparator, effect
  sizes, certainty, and clinical importance.

## Female-specific claims

- Do not promote cycle-synchronized nutrition or training from an influencer
  source.
- Search current consensus statements and female-specific systematic reviews.
- Prioritize adequate energy availability and individual symptoms; route
  disrupted menstruation and suspected low energy availability to qualified
  care.

- Editorial card: `editorial-clinician-books.md`
- Tracked: Kahn, *The Plant-Based Solution*; Newman, *The Plant Power Doctor*;

- Add another title only when a user actually raises it.
